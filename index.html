Why Burundi.

A small, mountainous country in the heart of Africa, Burundi grows coffee on family farms spread across its hills. Most of it is Red Bourbon, known for sweetness and balance.

Slow-ripened

Cool highland nights slow the cherry down. That is where the sweetness comes from.

Processed with care

Ripe cherries go to the station soon after picking, then are processed (washed, natural or another method, depending on the lot) and dried slowly.

Red fruitBlack teaCitrusBrown sugarFloralBright acidity

Typical notes for Burundi coffee. Each lot cups differently, so cupping details come with your samples.

From the hills to your warehouse.

Burundi is landlocked, so its coffee travels overland to an East African port before it goes to sea. We arrange each leg and send you the documents as it moves.

  1. BujumburaHulled, sorted and bagged at the dry mill.
  2. Dar es SalaamBy road or rail to the port, or to Mombasa.
  3. Montreal or HalifaxOcean freight to a Canadian or US port.
  4. Your warehouseWe clear customs and deliver in Ontario or where you need it.

Packed to keep the coffee fresh.

Green coffee ships in 60 kg jute bags, usually with a hermetic liner against moisture on the long voyage. A 20-foot container holds about 320 bags, roughly 19 tonnes. That full container is our minimum order.

FOB
Free on board, at the East African port. You book the ocean freight and take over once the coffee is loaded.
CIF
Cost, insurance and freight, to your port. We pay freight and insurance to a Canadian or US port. You clear customs.
DAP
Delivered at place. We bring the coffee to your door. You pay any import duties and taxes.

How an export works

  1. Send details. The product, how much you can supply and where you want to sell.
  2. Market check. We look at demand, import rules and labelling in the destination country.
  3. Find a buyer. We connect you with buyers and agree price and terms in writing.
  4. Ship it. We book the freight, prepare the documents and track the order to delivery.

Questions buyers ask.

How much coffee can you supply?

Tell us the volume you need, from one container to regular shipments, and we confirm availability, pricing and timing with you.

Can I order less than a full container?

No. Our minimum order is one full 20-foot container, about 320 bags or roughly 19 tonnes. Samples are available before you order.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the lot and the shipping route. We give you a timeline when we agree terms and keep you updated until it arrives.

Do you work with individuals or only businesses?

Both. We trade with businesses and with individual buyers. Green coffee is sold by the full container, so it suits importers, roasters and wholesalers. For other goods, tell us what you need and the quantity, and we will tell you whether we can supply it.

Do you sell roasted coffee?

No. We sell green, unroasted coffee to businesses that roast, blend or resell it.
